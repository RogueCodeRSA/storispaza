# M4 recording checklist — scripts must be approved in the app first

Exactly 24 clips, your consenting **adult** voice only. No child voices, cloning or synthetic Sesotho. The app normalizes uploads/recordings to `.wav`, 16 kHz mono PCM; choose a quiet room and no background music. Listen to every clip. Storage consent does not imply redistribution consent. Do not start recording a slot until its exact current script is approved in the app. Changing text invalidates dependent clips. The app's downloadable list contains exact current script text/version for all slots; pending text is blank and blocked.

Page narration covers the **narrative only**, not the heading, page number, illustration description or learning-moment sidebar. English source scripts below are provisional review copies, not approvals. Sesotho scripts will be the corresponding real-generated draft narratives after creator correction/approval. Vocabulary concepts selected in chat: light, story, electricity; exact Sesotho words and all call-and-response phrases must be supplied in the app.

## Provisional English source scripts

**EN01:** When the lights went out, a girl imagined a little glow above her hand. It was a storybook power, not something people can do in real life.

**EN02:** At school, she asked how a light could shine. Her teacher brought a small classroom battery kit. No one touched a wall socket or loose wire.

**EN03:** The teacher closed the kit's switch. Its tiny lamp lit up. A complete circuit makes a path for electric current, the teacher explained.

**EN04:** The girl thought about neighbours reading after dark. In her imaginary adventure, her glow could help. In everyday life, safe rechargeable lamps could help too.

**EN05:** That evening, families met to read. Adults set out charged lamps. In the story, the girl's little glow joined them. Everyone had a place around the table.

**EN06:** She could not change everything, but she could ask questions and help her neighbours. Tomorrow, she would bring another story to share.

Sesotho drafts and uncertain-term flags will appear beside these sources in the app. Only current app approvals establish recording readiness. This checklist is not a language review or completed fixture pack.
